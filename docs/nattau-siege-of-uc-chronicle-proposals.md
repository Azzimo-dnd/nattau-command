# Nattau Chronicle — proposed events after the Siege of UĆ

These are **drafts only**. They follow the existing public Chronicle format:
a short English headline, one compact factual sentence, and one established
tag. Do not publish them without GM selection/review. Events below were
provided directly by the GM following the session on 22 October 2026.

| Tag | Title | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Military | The Siege of UĆ | Imperial forces launched a devastating assault on UĆ while the heroes fought Lee Sin and forced him into the third phase of the battle. |
| Settlement | Fires Consume the Settlement | Most of UĆ's homes were set ablaze, and a fire in the storehouse destroyed part of the settlement's supplies. |
| Military | The Flying Galleon Lost | The flying galleon exploded after its core was struck during the siege, killing all sixty crew members aboard. |
| Military | Kealith Falls in Battle | Kealith, the ice dragoness, was killed during the Imperial assault on UĆ. |

| Arcane Incident | Kevin Returns as Nithis's Avenger | After Kevin fell in battle, Mother Tyllen restored him to life through the power of Nithis, driven by wrath and vengeance. |
| Military | The Vengeful Thirteen Are Formed | Kevin assembled twelve elite warriors of UĆ, training them in ruthless, rapid strikes with Imperial arms and armor refitted by the artificers. |
| Military | Ared's Tower Holds | Imperial attackers were repelled from Ared's tower without losses, thanks to newly trained recruits and artificer support. |
| Settlement | UĆ Begins to Rebuild | Despite heavy losses, Torsten's words at the funeral preserved the settlement's morale, while Pippo pledged workers and materials for reconstruction. |

## Editorial caveats
- The wider combat outcome against Lee Sin was **not** specified: do not write
  that Lee Sin was killed, defeated or captured.
- Roughly 40 civilian deaths includes the seven killed by booby-trapped bodies;
  the 60-person airship crew, five soldiers under KaRolK and Kealith are
  additional losses. The losses can be kept as a separate memorial entry if
  the GM prefers.
- The twelve warriors are living UĆ veterans; **Kevin is the returned avenger**.
  Do not call all thirteen undead or resurrected.
- Mira's fate is unknown: missing is not evidence of capture or betrayal.
- The new special unit begins **2/2 durability**, **0 RP cost** and no upkeep;
  no extra automatic combat bonuses were introduced into game rules.
